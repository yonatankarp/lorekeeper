Campaigns

Each campaign has its own notes folder

Switch campaigns at the top of the sidebar or from the tray menu. Remove only forgets a campaign here: its notes and backups stay where they are. A campaign is called after its folder unless you give it a name; the name shows in the sidebar and the tray, and each campaign backs up separately under it. A new name starts a new backup; the old one stays, and changing the name back carries it on. Tip: pick a folder inside your Obsidian vault or iCloud Drive.
